Imagine Dragons fan initiative

The Natural Project

A fan mission to push “Natural” to 3 Billion streams on Spotify and make Imagine Dragons the #1 band with the most 3B+ viewed songs on the platform.

Stream on Spotify Read the project

What is The Natural Project (TNP)?

Imagine Dragons already have 3 Billion streams on Believer, and are insanely close to reaching that milestone on Spotify with both Demons and Thunder that they are all but guaranteed to reach it within a few months. This would place them as equal for the band with the most songs to reach 3 Billion streams ever. But why do we want to be equal when we could be more? The Natural Project is our opportunity to push Imagine Dragons to that place.

Why Natural?

Natural has been the chosen song for 3 reasons. The reasons are listed below

  • Natural is already very popular. It is their eighth most streamed song and already halfway to the 3 Billion streams needed. This makes it more possible compared to other songs by this artist (even though I’d love to get Release and some of their more underrated stuff up there :( ).
  • Natural is more unique compared to other hits. For this project to work, we all have to stream the song. Having a more unique song with many more layers to their other work gives it more replay value, making the project feel less like a grind.
  • Imagine Dragons is constantly a bullied community for ‘simple songs’. I’m sure none of us want more of that. Natural is one of the more unique, less simple songs, and I haven’t really heard it get made fun of by non-Imagine Dragons fans to the same level as both songs above it (Thunder, Believer), and below it (On Top Of The World). This likely will cause minimal fun made compared to other, more popular songs like Enemy or Bones.

How can you contribute?

For The Natural Project, us fans will unite in the aims to get Natural to 3 Billion streams. And this is how we are going to do it!

Daily Streams

We are aiming for a minimum of 5 streams more than you usually would stream Natural every day for every participating Firebreather, along with double-stream Fridays (10 streams). This would put us in a very strong place, giving it an additional 40+ streams every week from just the participating members alone, not to mention the extra people who could hear it due to the algorithm promoting it again after the rapid stream growth. Please remember looping the song does not give it the streams, you must click on it individually and listen to it for at least 30 seconds.

The Playlist Rule

Aim to place Natural in every single playlist you own once per 5 songs there are. Therefore, when you’re shuffling their discography, other artists' music, or whatever else, you’re directly contributing to our rapid streams growth that we are aiming for.

Social Posts

When posting to your Instagram story or TikTok or whatever other social media you use, make sure to use this banger as the background music to as many of them as possible.

Spreading The Word

Spread the word across your socials, recommend your friends the amazing song and make sure that everyone knows that the 3B Natural is coming!

The Goal

The goal of this project, if it isn’t already clear enough, is to make Imagine Dragons the #1 band with the most 3B+ viewed songs on Spotify. If as many firebreathers as possible unite to this cause, we should hopefully be able to drag it to the top. Every stream counts, so go out there and never stop streaming!
